Athul Dileep

I'm a final year Ph.D. student at ARIES working under the supervision of Dr. Santosh Joshi, Scientist-F, ARIES. I have submitted my Ph.D. thesis to MJPRU, Bareilly on August 2026 and am awaiting my thesis defense. My research focuses on high-resolution spectroscopy and high-precision photometry of A- and F-type chemically peculiar stars. I mostly work with Am and Ap stars which shows pulsations, rotational variability and binarity. I use asteroseimology on the pulsating CP stars to understand their interiors.
